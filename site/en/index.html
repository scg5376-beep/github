Marketing in Korea, explained

5 articles · 357 official documents archived · every quote checked on every build · no numbers without a source

If you are bringing a product or a shop to Korea, most of what you know about search and social media still applies. Three things do not. Naver comes before Google.

Reviews feed the search engine directly. And the law asks for a few things before you sell. This guide covers those differences, using official documents wherever we could get them.

Where Koreans actually search

Numbers here carry the survey, the year and who was asked. Without those three, a number is not worth repeating. We checked each figure against the publisher's own page on 2026-09-18. A

ServiceUsed in the last 3 months (Opensurvey, AI Search Trend Report 2026 H1, 1,000 people aged 10 to 59, Dec 2025)
Naver81.6%
YouTube72.3%
ChatGPT54.5%
Gemini28.9%

Naver and YouTube are trending down and the AI services up. In the H2 report (July 2026) ChatGPT reached 58.1% and Gemini 52.2%. Still, when people look for everyday local information, 67.1% open Naver first. The direction is set. But today, most of your customers still start on Naver.

A plan that starts with Google and Instagram will miss most of them.

Generative AI use reached 44.5% of the whole population in 2025 (Ministry of Science and ICT survey, 50,750 people aged 3 and over). Among internet users it was 87.4% in 2026 (KT Nasmedia NPR; the publisher's summary does not state the sample size). Both are correct.

They asked different people. Keep the sample next to the number.

Sources: Opensurvey AI Search Trend Report 2026 H1 and H2 summary; KT Nasmedia 2026 NPR key findings; Ministry of Science and ICT, 2025 Survey on Internet Usage press release. All opened on 2026-09-18.
